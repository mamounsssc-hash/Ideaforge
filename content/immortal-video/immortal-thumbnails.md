## RESEARCH SUMMARY: What Makes a High-CTR Thumbnail

Based on analysis of top-performing channels (Kurzgesagt, CGP Grey, Zenn, What If, Pursuit of Wonder):

**Pattern 1 — Visual Paradox:** Show something impossible that forces the brain to resolve the contradiction (young face + old body, standing in your own grave)

**Pattern 2 — Emotional Isolation:** One small figure against a massive void/concept — creates both empathy and curiosity

**Pattern 3 — Dark Background + Bright Focal Point:** Nearly all top science/philosophy thumbnails use dark backgrounds (black, deep navy, dark purple) with one bright element that draws the eye

**Pattern 4 — Minimal Text (2-4 words MAX):** Large bold sans-serif, high contrast against background. Text creates the question, image creates the emotion

**Pattern 5 — Before/After or Split Composition:** Visual contrast between two states (young/old, happy/empty, alive/alone)

**Pattern 6 — Scale Contrast:** Tiny character vs. massive concept (time, universe, death) — creates feeling of insignificance that resonates with the topic

## TECHNICAL SPECS

- **File size:** Under 2MB
- **Format:** JPG or PNG
- **Text:** Max 4 words, 80pt+ font, sans-serif (Impact, Bebas Neue, or Montserrat Black)
- **Safe zone:** Keep critical elements away from bottom-right corner (YouTube timestamp overlay) and bottom-left (video duration)
